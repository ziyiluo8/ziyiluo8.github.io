Inside Chrometry

Precision Behind Every Spool

Professional 3D printing materials require control at every stage—from formulation and color development to extrusion and final inspection.

Material Development

Performance starts with the material.

Before a filament reaches the production line, its material behavior has to be understood.

We focus on formulation, processing conditions, extrusion stability, and print performance to develop materials that behave predictably from spool to finished part.

Color Development

Vivid by design.

Color is a core part of how we develop materials.

We refine saturation, tone, visual depth, and color transitions while evaluating how each formulation appears after printing—not just how it looks on the spool.

The goal is bold, expressive color with the consistency required for reliable printing.

Controlled Production

Consistency is built into the process.

Material preparation, extrusion, cooling, diameter control, winding, and spooling work as one connected process.

Careful control at each stage helps maintain stable flow, dimensional consistency, feeding reliability, and repeatable material characteristics.

Built to Scale. Designed for Consistency.

Behind every Chrometry spool is a manufacturing system built for precision, stability, and repeatability.

With advanced production capabilities and continuous process optimization, we are equipped to support the development and production of high-quality 3D printing materials.
